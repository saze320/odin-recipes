Fried Rice

Description

This is typical Nigerian dish rich in natural herbs and spices. It is often accompanied with proteins(most especially fried chicken and fish), coleslaw etc.

A sumptous and pallatable delicacy, eaten with joy and happiness. It is mostly enjoyed with family or with a company of friends.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken
  • Carrots
  • Green peas
  • Green beans
  • Sweet corn
  • Green bell pepper
  • Spring onions
  • Onion
  • curry & Thyme
  • salt
  • Stock cubes
  • Chicken broth
  • Liver

Steps

  1. Cook the rice with chicken stock and little salt until almost done;drain and set aside.

  2. Cook the chicken with onion,seasoning,and water; reserve the stock.

  3. Fry the vegetables (carrots, peas, green beans, sweet corn and pepper briefly in oil).

  4. Add seasoning-curry powder,thyme,stock cube,and a little salt.

  5. Add the cooked rice and stir-fry everything together.

  6. Add some chicken stock if needed, and then stir until the rice is well combined and slightly dry.

  7. Add cooked liver and stir for another 2-3 minutes

  8. Taste and adjust seasoning, then serve.
