Coconut Rice

Description

A feshly made delicacy with coconut extract and other rich natural herbs and ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Coconut milk
  • Vegetable oil
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • hicken or stock cube
  • Chicken stock or water
  • Curry powder,Thyme,Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Mixed vegetables (carrot,peas,sweet corn)
  • Green pepper
  • Spring onions

Steps

  1. Wash and parboil the rice until partly cooked;drain.

  2. Fry onion,garlic,ginger and pepper in oil.

  3. Add curry,thyme,seasoning ad salt;stir briefly.

  4. Add coconut milk and chickrn stock/water and bring to a boil.

  5. Add the parboiled rice,stir,and cover.

  6. Cook on low heat until the rice is tender and liquid is absorbed.

  7. Add vegetables near the end and mix gently.

  8. Taste and adjust seasoning,then serve.
