Jollof Rice

Description

Jollof Rice is a dish that is common among the West Africans. It is deeply revered due to its pleasing aroma and spicy flavor.

It is often accompanied with rich source of protein food (fried Fish and beans), fried plantain or coleslaw.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of parboiled rice
  • Fresh tomato
  • Fresh Pepper
  • Sachet or Tin tomato paste
  • Ginger & Garlic
  • onions
  • Curry spices
  • Thyme
  • seasoning cubes & Salt
  • Vegetable oil

Steps

  1. Wash and blend the vegetables

    2. Blend the tomatoes, pepper and one onion until smooth

  2. Parboil the rice

    3. Wash the rice and parboil it briefly. Drain and set aside.

  3. Fry the Onions

    4. Heat the oil in a large and add the oil for few minutes.

  4. Fry the tomato paste

    5. Add the tomato paste and fry for about 2-3 minutes, stiring so it doesn't burn.

  5. Add the blended pepper mix

    6. Pour it in carefully and cook on medium heat until the mixture thickens and the excess water reduces.

  6. Season the sauce

    7. Add thyme,curry powder,seasoning cubes and salt.

  7. Add the rice

    8. Put the parboiled rice into the sauce and mix thoroughly so the sauce coats the rice.

  8. Add liquid and cook

    9. Add enough water to the rice, cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat.

  9. Check as it cooks

    10. After about 15-20 minutes, check the rice. If it's still hard and the liquid has dried up, add a small amount of water and continue cooking.

  10. Finish

    11. Once the rice is tender, gently stir it and allow to steam for another few minutes.
